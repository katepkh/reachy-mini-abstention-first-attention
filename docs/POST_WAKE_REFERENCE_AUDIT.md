# Post-wake identity-reference audit

## Question

The controlled startup series found a repeatable 2.529–2.752° mean rotation
from identity. Before interpreting that result, this audit asks:

> Is identity actually the head-pose endpoint that Reachy Mini daemon 1.9.0
> intends after its normal wake animation?

This distinction matters. A wrong project reference would invalidate the
preflight comparison; a correct reference with a measured residual leaves a
target-tracking, calibration, kinematic, or mechanical question.

## Version-pinned source trace

The released daemon 1.9.0 source answers the reference question:

1. [`AbstractBackend.INIT_HEAD_POSE`](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini/blob/v1.9.0/src/reachy_mini/daemon/backend/abstract.py#L1108-L1124)
   is a 4×4 identity matrix.
2. [`wake_up()`](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini/blob/v1.9.0/src/reachy_mini/daemon/backend/abstract.py#L1134-L1161)
   requests that pose, performs the visible roll, and requests identity again
   as its final motion.
3. Normal daemon startup enables the motors and awaits that wake sequence in
   [`daemon.py`](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini/blob/v1.9.0/src/reachy_mini/daemon/daemon.py#L346-L360).
4. The physical backend writes its retained target joint positions on each
   enabled control-loop update in
   [`robot/backend.py`](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini/blob/v1.9.0/src/reachy_mini/daemon/backend/robot/backend.py#L184-L205).

Therefore identity is the correct **source-defined wake endpoint** for this
software version. The project's 1° acceptance tolerance is a separate,
conservative experimental boundary. It is not a Pollen Robotics tolerance,
manufacturer limit, or calibration specification.

## What the Testbench `OK` results establish

The earlier Testbench screenshots showed that every discovered motor matched
the expected return delay, operating mode, angle limits, homing offset, and
shutdown configuration. The Testbench's configuration-check implementation
compares those EEPROM fields with the hardware configuration. That is useful,
but it does not compare the daemon's live Cartesian/joint target with measured
position and does not certify geometric assembly or pose convergence.

Thus `OK` motor configuration and a repeatable post-wake pose residual are not
contradictory observations.

## Verdict and remaining uncertainty

**Resolved:** the project did not choose the wrong nominal pose. Identity is the
daemon 1.9.0 wake endpoint, and the non-identity result exists in both Cartesian
pose and measured joint state. Its repeatability makes random forward-kinematic
noise an insufficient explanation.

**Not resolved:** source inspection shows what wake intends to request, but the
released `FullState` schema drops the target fields that its route tries to
return. The captures therefore cannot prove which target remained stored 60
seconds later. Without simultaneous present-versus-target telemetry, the
residual cannot honestly be labeled tracking error, encoder/homing error,
geometric miscalibration, mechanical deflection, cable load, or damage.

**Additional exclusion:** every controlled capture reported motor mode
`enabled` before and after sampling, zero control-loop errors, and no daemon or
backend error. Disabled torque and a reported control-loop fault are therefore
contradicted for the sampled intervals, although this does not distinguish the
remaining target, tracking, model, assembly, or load explanations.

**Decision:** keep the 1° project gate unchanged, send no V4 motion, and do not
use the rejected custom-centring proposal. The minimal target-field schema
repair now passes extracted-route and complete isolated mockup-daemon REST and
WebSocket tests described in
[`TARGET_STATE_OBSERVABILITY.md`](TARGET_STATE_OBSERVABILITY.md), but it must
not be installed on this robot until independently reviewed under a separate,
reversible deployment protocol.

The resulting [`maintenance triage`](MAINTENANCE_TRIAGE.md) preserves that
stop decision and separates read-only evidence from owner-authorized invasive
inspection.
