# M5 fused shadow controller

Status: **SOFTWARE PROTOTYPE — NO ROBOT OR RESPONSE AUTHORITY**

The M5 controller is the first single decision interface over the existing
spatial-compatibility and audio–mouth-synchrony components. It consumes only
already-derived numeric evidence and emits exactly one of:

- `SELECT`: one already-visible face track is a candidate for later attention;
- `HOLD`: evidence is still accumulating; or
- `ABSTAIN`: evidence is conflicting, invalid, stale, unavailable, or explicitly negative.

Every output has `motion_authorized=false`, `response_authorized=false`, zero
actuation commands, and zero response commands. `SELECT` therefore means only
“eligible for the next shadow evaluation.” It is not liveness, speaker identity,
intent to address the robot, mutual gaze, or permission to move or speak.

## Deterministic fusion contract

The first prototype selects only when both inputs are fresh, close enough in
time, support the same non-null face-track identifier, and the spatial heading
is finite and within the shadow interface range. Pending evidence holds. Any
source abstention, conflict, unavailability, unhealthy runtime state, stale
timestamp, cross-source clock skew, face mismatch, or invalid heading abstains.

Reason precedence is fail-closed and deterministic: health and structural
validity, timestamp validity, conflicts, source abstentions, unavailability,
pending evidence, cross-modal identity-of-track agreement, then heading scope.

The exact implementation is
[`reachy_stage3v/fused_shadow.py`](../reachy_stage3v/fused_shadow.py). The
deterministic synthetic truth table is generated by
[`scripts/run_fused_shadow_contract.py`](../scripts/run_fused_shadow_contract.py).

## Scope fixed by the first working version

- The candidate must already be visible in the camera field of view. Speech
  outside the view abstains; no search motion is permitted. Bounded search is a
  separate future capability.
- Audio–lip synchrony is association evidence under the tested playback
  conditions, not a general liveness detector. A synchronized talking-face
  video, deliberate lip-sync, or other coordinated replay may pass it.
- Face-directed centring is not eye contact. Speaking is not proof that a person
  addressed Reachy. Those questions require separate interaction definitions
  and evidence.

## Data flow and privacy boundary

The fusion module receives timestamps, one ephemeral face-track label, a face
heading, status/reason codes, correlation, and lag. It receives no pixels,
waveform, transcript, embedding, identity, or name. The current browser pilots
derive numeric audio level and face landmarks locally on the laptop; their raw
camera/audio samples exist transiently in the browser/runtime and are not saved
or uploaded by the research recorder. Reachy's future integrated instrument
must separately document which samples are transient on the robot and which
derived fields cross to the laptop.

## Evidence gap before M6

No saved dataset currently contains spatial direction, face track, and
synchrony features on the same trial clock. Stage 3V records spatial geometry
but no lip signal; the V2 pilot records audio level and lip aperture but no DoA
or face heading. Pairing unrelated trials would fabricate joint evidence.

The next critical deliverable is therefore a command-free joint numeric
instrument, followed by a small frozen shadow study that measures both live
visible speech and silent-visible-person plus phone playback at matched face
visibility, distance, duration, and signal-quality eligibility. The study must
report acquisition failures and latency as outcomes, and include official-style
DoA, acoustic-only, visual-only, synchrony-only, fused non-abstaining, and fused
abstaining baselines.
